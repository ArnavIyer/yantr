camera_color_optical_frame uses the optical convention from the Astra driver: +X right, +Y down, +Z forward.

Display Mapping

URDF/ROS frames are right-handed: +X forward, +Y left, +Z up. This viewer maps them into Three.js display coordinates as ROS X -> Three -Z, ROS Y -> Three -X, and ROS Z -> Three +Y. Frame axes keep their ROS labels and colors.
